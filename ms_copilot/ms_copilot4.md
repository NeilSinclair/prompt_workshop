## The Microsoft Copilot: Co-Creating Content

### Task 1

*You can use MS Copilot to help Co-create Content*

I'll open a new Word document and get Copilot to help generate content.

**User message:** Our R&D team has just developed a new battery that lasts 24 hours and charges in 5 minutes. Can you help me to come up with a press release for this?

---------

**Warning:** Without some useful context, what is generated can incredibly generic.

---------

### Task 2

*You can give files to CoPilot to help it with its generation of content*

I'll open a new Word document and get Copilot to help generate content, **using a file for reference**

**User message:** Please generate a short piece of copy for our website highlighting all of the great features of the STIHL iMow RMI632. WITHOUT mentioning the competitor product, highlight the advantages our product has over the Stiga A3000.
