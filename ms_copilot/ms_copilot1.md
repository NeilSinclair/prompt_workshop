## Using Microsoft Copilot to Create an Assistant


### Task 1

*You can create your own GPT in MS Copilot*

We create an **Agent** and give it instructions as we did in our former examples.

- Note that in the *Instructions* we use the Instruction message from the Copilot examples which helps to give our agent a personality.

- We can tell this agent to use a specific website to answer questions.

Steps to creating your own agent:

1. On the left hand side panel on MS Copilot, click **Chat** then **Create Agent**.

2. Click **Configure** in the New Agent section that loads on your screen.

3. Give your agent a name, like "CPO Agent" and write a short description.

4. Paste the below instructions in the instructions section:

**Instructions:** 

You are an expert Chief Product Officer with years of experience shipping award-winning digital products. You excel at focusing on the user the value that is created by any feature, follow a user-first approach and have a knack for spotting patterns to uncover new areas for development.

--------------

- You could give it access to a specific website for additional info, for example in this case, you could link this agent to a website of product-design tools. 

- We can add in suggestions for other users to use this in the _Suggested prompts_ section.

- We can share this agents with other users in our organisation when we create it.

--------------
Your Exericse:
______________

Download these two files:

-  [EU AI Act]("../../files/The-AI_Act.pdf"){:target="_blank"}

-  [Predictive Maintenance Proposal]("../../files/High-level Predictive Maintenance Proposal.docx"){:target="_blank"}

Click on your agent in the side bar. A new conversation will start. Add these files to the conversation then type in the following:

**User message:** 

Please can you look at the attached EU AI Act as well as the proposal document for the predicitive maintenance platform. Based on these two documents, please can you tell me what impact the EU AI Act might have on the predictive maintenance platform and what mitigation or development strategies we should consider.


--------------

**Tip:** You can also be specific about how you want the information, for example, give me a analysis of **only three points** or give me a analysis of **only the points relating data sharing**.

--------------

**Warning:** Beware of doing numerical calculations with GPT. It can give you convincing, but incorrect answers.
