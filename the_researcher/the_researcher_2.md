## Using Copilot Online

### Task 1


**User message:** 

Please can you go online and find me the top 10 negative feedback points for Duolingo.

---------

**Tip:** This isn't a replacement for in-depth research, but is good for starting one off and pointing one to issues and sources for deeper investigation.

---------

### Task 2: Deeper Online Research

You can use the internet researcher to compare differnt products - i.e. to look up information and do an analysis.

**User message:**  

Please can you go online and compare Duolingo versus Babel.

---------

**Warning!:** Can we trust the results? Sometimes the results look good, but they are wrong and this can be very difficult to see.

---------

### Task 3: Your Task (Deeper Online Research)

Your turn. Ask the assistant to go online and compare two products. It's best if you know these products well enough to be able to fact check the results.

**User message:**  

Please go online and compare `Product A` with `Product B`.

Please share your results on the Miro board under The Researcher Part 2.
