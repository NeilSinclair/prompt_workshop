## Using Bing Copilot Online

### Task 1


**User message:** 

Please can you pull the top 10 negative reviews for the STIHL iMow RMI 632 PC? This is in preparation for a marketing meeting I have soon.

**Tip:** This isn't a replacement for in-depth research, but is good for starting one off and pointing one to issues and sources for deeper investigation.
